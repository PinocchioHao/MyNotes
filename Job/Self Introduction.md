###  The internet of things
The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to a network of interconnected devices that can collect, share, and act on data through the internet. These devices, ranging from household appliances(家电) to industrial machines, are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies, enabling them to communicate and interact with each other and external systems. IoT enhances automation, efficiency, and real-time monitoring in various applications, such as smart homes, healthcare, transportation, and agriculture. This interconnected(相互连接的) ecosystem(生态系统) is changing the way we live and work, providing new insights and improving decision-making processes.
