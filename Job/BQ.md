### How have you persuaded someone to see things your way at work?

这里提我需求采用的模块化开发的结构，抽出公共逻辑供大家调用，并且按业务模块进行设计，原有的代码的逻辑是多个业务写在一大块里面，耦合比较严重... 我的代码在大家评审看起来就很清爽，易于维护

While I was developing a new feature, I used a **modular approach**. I **extracted the shared logic into Common Services** and organized the rest into separate **business modules**.

During the code review, the team was impressed. My code looked much **cleaner** compared to the old structure, and they realized it would be much easier to maintain and test in the future.

As a result, they agreed to adopt this **modular structure** as our new reference. For all the following requirements, the team started following this pattern to make our development more **consistent and scalable**.


// 进一步追问我的模板回答：就是Controller->Service->ServiceImpl->Dao 这种架构（需要提到设计模式吗？），只是我基于模块抽出了一些公共service，业务service，规定了新加的接口应该写在哪，接口之间的层级关系等，使代码简洁易懂，易于维护...
The structure follows the standard **Controller -> Service -> DAO** layers, but the key improvement was how I organized the logic.
